    Our story

    Bridging design intent and site reality.

    Infrigidate Engineers India Pvt. Ltd. was established in 2024 in Pune by professionals from high-tech HVAC infrastructure projects. We have since extended operations to Madhya Pradesh with an office at Pithampur, and today a team of 25+ professionals delivers projects across India.

    Our operating model

    • Design-assist mindset supporting clients, consultants and PMCs
    • Strong engineering ownership from early project stages
    • Single-point execution responsibility
    • Integrated coordination of allied services
    • Continued involvement beyond commissioning and handover
    Where we add maximum value

    The projects others find hard.

    • Design collaboration with simulation studies for precision environment conditions
    • Schedule-sensitive and coordination-intensive projects
    • Projects requiring strong documentation discipline
    • Facilities where long-term performance matters
    • Compliance-driven and regulated facilities
